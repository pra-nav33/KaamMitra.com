How KaamMitra Works

Getting your work done is simple, safe and convenient.

👤 For Customers

Find the right worker and get your work completed easily.

01

Find a Service

Choose the service you need from our available categories and subcategories.

02

Choose a Worker

Compare workers based on rating, experience, location, price and availability.

03

Send Request / Book

Select your preferred worker, date, time and location and send a service request.

04

Get the Work Done

The worker completes your requested service.

05

Pay & Review

Complete the payment and leave a rating and review for the worker.

🧑‍🔧 For Workers

Join KaamMitra and grow your service business.

01

Create Your Profile

Register as a professional and create your worker profile.

02

Add Your Services

Select your categories, services, experience and pricing.

03

Receive Job Requests

Customers near you can find your profile and send service requests.

04

Accept & Complete Jobs

Accept suitable requests and complete the customer's work professionally.

05

Get Paid & Reviewed

Receive payment and build your reputation through customer reviews.
